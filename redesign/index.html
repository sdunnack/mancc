Welcome to

Manchester
Country Club

Manchester Country Club is a Tillinghast-redesigned 18-hole course set in the rolling hills of Manchester, Connecticut. Named a GolfPass Top 50 Public Course from over 226,000 golfer reviews, MCC offers challenging, well-conditioned play for golfers of every level — along with newly upgraded practice facilities and a welcoming clubhouse community.

Our Facilities

Newly Upgraded Practice Facilities

In addition to the course itself, MCC now offers an 18-bay driving range and a 3,000 sq ft short-game practice green and chipping area — along with indoor simulator bays open year-round.

Whether you're a beginner or a seasoned player, Manchester Country Club is the place to sharpen your game and enjoy it.

Named a GolfPass Top 50 Public Course

Originally designed by Tom Bendelow and Deveroux Emmet in 1917 and redesigned by A. W. Tillinghast in 1935, MCC was recognized by GolfPass as a Top 50 Public Course based on 226,000+ golfer reviews.
