Course History

A Course With a Pedigree

Manchester Country Club was originally designed by Tom Bendelow and Deveroux Emmet in 1917, then redesigned by the legendary A. W. Tillinghast in 1935 — a legacy that still shapes every hole today. In recognition of the course's condition and character, MCC was named a GolfPass Top 50 Public Course based on more than 226,000 golfer reviews.

Take a virtual tour of the grounds below, from the newly upgraded practice facilities to the clubhouse and course itself.
